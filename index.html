Privacy Policy for Digital Tasbee or Counter

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Counter app ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. Counter is developed by Salman Padder (MSP Store). We respect your privacy: the App is designed to work entirely on your device and does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. Counter does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, or any other personal information, and does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking technologies.

Data Stored on Your Device

The App saves your settings locally on your device using Android's SharedPreferences. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us or to any third party. It includes:

  • Your counter values.
  • Your custom button labels.
  • The number of counters you choose to display.
  • Your haptic (vibration) feedback preference and intensity.

This information is removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. If device backup is enabled in your Android settings, these preferences may be included in your personal device/cloud backup, which is controlled by you and your operating system — not by us.

Permissions

Counter requests a single permission:

  • Vibrate (android.permission.VIBRATE) — used only to provide optional haptic feedback when you tap a button. It does not access any personal data.

Links to Other Services

The App may contain a link to its listing on the Google Play Store (for example, to let you rate the App). If you follow such a link, you leave the App and the privacy policy of that external service applies. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of external services.

Children's Privacy

Counter does not collect any data from anyone, including children. The App is suitable for general audiences and contains no data collection, ads, or in-app purchases.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
